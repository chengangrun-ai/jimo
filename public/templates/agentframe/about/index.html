A NOTE ABOUT THIS PLACE

A fictional brand.
A real starting point.

Made for your next good idea.

Agentframe is a premium website template from React Bits Pro. It imagines an AI harness with tools, memory, checkpoints, and human approvals. Its product, pricing, customer names, company marks, and testimonials are fictional demonstration content.

The interactive agent runs use local sample data. Code examples describe a fictional SDK and are intended to illustrate the product, not to install or connect a live service. Plan buttons open a local preview; they do not create accounts or collect payments.

Privacy, without the small print.

This template has no analytics, tracking pixels, authentication, newsletter collection, or payment processing. Your theme and motion preferences are saved in your browser. Copy buttons write code examples to your clipboard only when you choose to copy them.

The landscape is served with this template. Article photographs load from Unsplash through the site’s image service. External links take you to services with their own privacy policies. When you adapt this template into a live product, replace this page with terms and privacy information that reflect your actual services.

Terms of this demonstration.

Prices and capabilities are illustrative, not an offer to provide a service. Template usage is governed by the React Bits Pro commercial license included with the source files. This page is explanatory content for the demonstration, not a legal policy for a live business.
