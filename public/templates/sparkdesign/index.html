Start with whatever is on your mind: a brand direction, a product idea, a presentation, or a collection of references. Sparkdesign is a shared space for bringing the pieces together.

The product concept includes shared project links and clear viewing and editing permissions, so collaborators can join just the work you invite them into. The previews on this page demonstrate those controls locally.

This demo runs right in your browser. Explore the canvas, try the feedback controls, and switch between versions without an account or a download.

You’ll see a local preview with the name you choose. Nothing is sent or saved to a server, and no account is created. Refreshing the page resets the example.
